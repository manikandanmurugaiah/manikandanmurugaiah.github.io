ABOUT

Academic Profile

I am an Associate Professor in the School of Aerospace Engineering at Ramaiah Institute of Science and Management (RISM), Bengaluru. My academic work brings together aerospace systems, computational methods, data-driven modelling and engineering education.

My research focuses on aircraft design, lighter-than-air systems, unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs), multidisciplinary design optimization, computational fluid dynamics, and artificial intelligence for aerospace applications. I am particularly interested in using multidisciplinary design and computational approaches to address complex aerospace engineering problems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Aerospace Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Bombay in 2022. My doctoral research focused on the design optimization of tri-lobed lifting-body Dynastat airships. I also hold a Master's degree in Astronomy and Space Engineering from Manipal Institute of Technology and a Bachelor's degree in Aeronautical Engineering from MLR Institute of Technology, JNTU.

Over more than 16 years in engineering education, I have taught and mentored students across aircraft design, airship technology, aerodynamics, flight dynamics, UAV design, aircraft performance, spaceflight mechanics, computational methods and related aerospace subjects.

RESEARCH

Research & Innovation

My research integrates aircraft design, lighter-than-air systems and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) with multidisciplinary design optimization, computational fluid dynamics and artificial intelligence for aerospace applications.

01

Aircraft Design

Conceptual and preliminary design of aerospace vehicles with emphasis on aerodynamic performance, configuration development and engineering trade-offs.

02

Lighter-than-Air Systems

Design, analysis and optimization of stratospheric, hybrid and multi-lobed airships and other lighter-than-air configurations.

03

Unmanned Aerial Vehicles (UAVs)

UAV configuration development, aerodynamic analysis, long-endurance platforms and emerging aerospace applications.

04

Multidisciplinary Design Optimization

Integrated optimization of aerospace systems by considering interacting disciplines, design variables, constraints and performance objectives.

05

Computational Fluid Dynamics

Numerical investigation of aerodynamic flows and computational methods for aerospace vehicle analysis and design.

06

Artificial Intelligence for Aerospace Applications

Application of artificial intelligence and machine learning methods to aerospace design, analysis, prediction and optimization.
